## Design and delivery

The [identity decision](comparison-identity-decision-2026-10-29.md) separates exact occurrences, future logical identity/revisions, translation relations and publication lineage. String lists use exact occurrences; rewriting means removal plus addition. Scoped object keys must be unique. Other object collections match complete exact values, never an incidental identifier. Root, chain and causal locations are preserved.

The [FR guide](../docs/fr/pack-comparison.md) and [EN guide](../docs/en/pack-comparison.md) specify admission, matching, report envelope, export, limits and private CLI. jsondiffpatch 0.7.6 is confined to the optional artifact. Strings are atomic with textDiff.minLength Infinity, including after shared text-engine initialization; delta values are cloned. No patch, unpatch, reverse or HTML formatter is exposed or invoked. Ajv 8.20.0 validates both pinned schemas after whole-snapshot verification. The reference verifier, four historic schemas, digest recipes, existing semantic fixtures and T3 evidence remain unchanged.

Comparison uses a separate session/epoch and cancellation signal. Closing, changing selection or disconnecting clears the usable result. Two bounded workers maximum, 10 seconds each, contain computation. Report and operation limits refuse instead of truncating. The original full-read pending lock and exact pack projection remain independent. The UI catalog offers only the retained and new fictional states; the CLI handles explicitly configured private originals offline.

All changed fields remain in scope: pack, manifest, selected units and archived source bytes. Original pack/manifest base64 and complete context are retained. Raw branches must normalize or fail; all operation IDs occur exactly once in the grouped report and rendered items. Filter totals identify operations, not distinct normative rules. Source text is decoded for inspection without making it executable or replacing the bytes.
